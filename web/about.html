Cube Labeler

A free web app for designing and printing labels on the Brother PT-P300BT (P-touch Cube) label printer. It runs in Chrome or Edge on Windows, Mac, Linux and Android and prints over Bluetooth, with nothing to install and no account needed.

Open Cube Labeler

What it does

  • Text labels with bold, italic, underlined and inverted text, sizes per line, columns, frames, QR codes and pictures.
  • Bin labels for screws, bolts and parts: size, name, lengths and a drive or part icon.
  • A print queue that prints several labels as one strip, to save tape.
  • An icon library and icon editor: turn a photo into a crisp black and white icon.
  • Templates and backups, so your labels, icons and settings move between devices.
  • A live preview that shows exactly how the label prints, dot for dot.

Google Drive sync (optional)

You can sign in with Google to keep your own icons, templates and settings in step across your devices. Cube Labeler then saves backups in a Cube Labeler backups folder in your Google Drive and reads them on your other devices. It only asks for access to the files it creates itself, and can't see anything else in your Drive. Everything else in the app works without signing in. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Who makes it
