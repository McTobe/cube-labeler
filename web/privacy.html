Privacy

Cube Labeler web · last updated 26 October 2026

Cube Labeler designs and prints labels on the Brother PT-P300BT label printer. It is a hobby project, free to use, with its source code on GitHub.

What stays on your device

Your labels, print queue, icons, templates, settings and added fonts are kept in your browser on your own device. The app draws labels and talks to the printer inside the browser. None of this is sent to the Cube Labeler server.

If you add an API key for an AI service (Gemini, OpenAI or OpenRouter), it also stays in your browser. It is sent only to that service, when you ask it to redraw a picture, and never goes in backups.

Google Drive sync

If you choose Sign in with Google, the app asks Google for one permission: to see and change only the files it makes itself (the drive.file scope). It cannot see any other file in your Drive. It uses this to save backups of your icons, templates and settings in a Cube Labeler backups folder, and to read them back on your other devices. It also reads your account's email address to show which account is signed in.

To keep you signed in, Google gives the Cube Labeler server a sign-in key (a refresh token). The server does not store it: it is encrypted and kept in a cookie in your own browser, and the server uses it only to get a new short-lived Drive pass for your browser when it asks. Your backups go straight from your browser to Google and never pass through the server.

Cube Labeler's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Your data is not sold, shared with anyone or used for advertising.

Signing out and removing data

Sign out in Settings removes the cookie and tells Google to cancel the sign-in key. You can also remove Cube Labeler's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. The backups are ordinary files in your Drive, so you can delete the folder whenever you like. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything the app kept on that device.

Server logs

Like any web server, the server that hosts the app may briefly log requests (address, time and page) to keep it running. It uses no analytics, no ads and no tracking cookies.
