Privacy policy

Cube Labeler web · last updated 26 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Cube Labeler web app at print.tobezone.com handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. Cube Labeler is a free app for designing and printing labels on the Brother PT-P300BT label printer, made by McTobe (contact: admin@tobezone.com). See About Cube Labeler for what the app does.

Summary

  • Your labels, icons, templates and settings stay in your own browser.
  • If you turn on Google Drive sync, backups go to your own Google Drive, and the app can only see the files it created there.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Information the app handles

  • What you make in the app: label text, pictures, icons, templates, the print queue, settings and added fonts. These are stored in your browser's storage on your device. They are not sent to the Cube Labeler server.
  • AI service keys (optional): if you add an API key for Gemini, OpenAI or OpenRouter, it is stored in your browser. It is sent only to that service, together with the picture you ask it to redraw, and never goes in backups.
  • Google account data (optional, only if you sign in with Google), described below.
  • Server logs: like any web server, the server may log technical request data (IP address, time, the page requested and the browser's user agent) to keep the service running and secure.

The app uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies.

Google user data

Signing in with Google is optional and is used only for Google Drive sync. When you sign in, Cube Labeler asks for one permission, the drive.file scope: see, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app. It cannot see or change any other file in your Drive.

  • What it accesses: the files it creates itself, which are a Cube Labeler backups folder and the .cubebackup files in it (your own icons, templates and settings), and your account's email address, which it reads to show which account is signed in.
  • How it is used: to save a backup when your icons, templates or settings change, to read the newest backup on your other devices, to keep the newest 10 backups and one a day for two weeks and move older ones it made to your Drive's bin, and to let you restore a backup you pick. Each backup is labelled with the kind of device that made it (for example Android or Windows) and how many icons and templates it holds. It is used for nothing else.
  • Where it goes: backups go straight between your browser and Google Drive. They do not pass through the Cube Labeler server and are not stored there.
  • Staying signed in: so you don't have to sign in every hour, Google gives the Cube Labeler server a refresh token when you sign in. The server does not store it. It is encrypted and kept in a cookie in your own browser (HttpOnly, Secure, sent only to the sign-in part of the site), and the server uses it only to get a new short-lived Drive access token for your browser when the app asks for one.
  • Sharing: Google user data is not shared with, transferred to or sold to anyone, is not used for advertising, and is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models.

Cube Labeler's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

  • Data in your browser stays until you delete it. Clearing the site's data in your browser settings removes all of it from that device.
  • Backups in your Google Drive are ordinary files that you own. You can delete the Cube Labeler backups folder at any time.
  • Sign out in Settings removes the sign-in cookie from that browser. Remove access on all devices also asks Google to revoke the refresh token, which signs out every device. You can also remove Cube Labeler's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Server logs are used only to keep the service running, and are deleted when they are no longer needed for that.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS only. The sign-in cookie is encrypted and signed, and it can't be read by scripts on the page.

Children

Cube Labeler is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
